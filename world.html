World

Crystalleum

The world of Crystallem was almost destroyed by the Fifth Crystal War which caused millions of deaths across all nations. Because of all the harm that this war caused, three of the most powerful nations of Crystalleum decided to form a truce. They were the kingdom of Lukia, the kingdom of Anarov and lastly, the revolutionary country of Gernim. With the help of the most powerful men among the three nations, the Crystalline Order was created. At its head is Archmage Elyseus. Elyseus wishes to maintain global peace with his organization…

Crystals and Magic

Crystals in the world of Crystalleum are made up of concentrated mana. They often manifest where magic flows the most. Archmages and mages of old used to channel and conduct magic, and often, those crystals would appear where the former used their powers. Thus, there is a strong connection between crystals and magic, the crystals being the product of the usage of mana over time. Magic does come in many forms however, and so do the crystals that result from them.
